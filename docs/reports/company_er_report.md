# Real companies across three systems (Wikidata, SEC EDGAR, GLEIF)

Generated by `scripts/company_er_benchmark.py`. Each US-listed company with both an SEC CIK and an LEI in Wikidata appears as a CRM-like record (Wikidata: everyday name, website, HQ city), a finance-like record (SEC EDGAR: filing name, street address, phone) and a legal-entity record (GLEIF: legal name, address), plus a stale CRM account under the company's most recent former name when it changed name. All values are real (CC0 / public domain); the answer key is the identifiers, which Canon does not see.

## dev (1033 companies, 3487 records)

| method | precision | recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| exact normalized name | 0.991 | 0.618 | 0.761 |
| Canon (pairwise) | 0.975 | 0.741 | 0.842 |
| Canon (B-cubed) | 0.990 | 0.825 | 0.900 |

- Companies whose records all ended up together: 664 of 1033 (64%).
- Clusters that wrongly mix two companies: 18 (counted as errors even when the answer key itself has two Wikidata items for one company).
- Renamed companies: the stale account under the former name was linked for 299 of 385; 74 more were sent to a person.
- Review queue: 1320 pair decisions.
