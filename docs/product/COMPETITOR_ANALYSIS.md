# Competitor analysis

*Part of the product document set. Target market: the US first, then the UK and EU (decision 94). Market statements are hypotheses unless a source is linked; no customer has been interviewed. Written by the agent that built most of the code. Updated 2026-10-31.*

| Alternative | What it does well | Where Canon differs | Likely response |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRM-native AI and data tools (Salesforce Data Cloud / Agentforce, HubSpot data quality and AI) | Native, already licensed | Reconciles the CRM *with* contracts and Slack/Teams, per fact, with sources; runs outside the vendor's cloud | "Use our features"; partners may resist or resell |
| Enterprise search with permissions (Glean, Microsoft Copilot) | Permission-aware retrieval of documents, many connectors | Returns *reconciled facts* with confidence and conflicts, plus a CRM fix list; no per-seat platform | Ignore at this size |
| MCP gateways / registries (e.g. MintMCP, CData) | Access control and inventory for agent tools | Canon fixes the data the tools return, and enforces per-caller ACL inside the answer | Possible partner |
| Data-quality / MDM platforms (Informatica, Reltio, Tamr) | Enterprise master data | Two weeks, not a programme; affordable for mid-market | Not present in this segment |
| CRM dedupe tools (e.g. Cloudingo, DemandTools, Dedupely) | Cheap duplicate merging inside the CRM | Cross-source truth, renewals from contracts, agent permissions | Overlap on duplicates only |
| RevOps consultancies | Trust and relationships | Repeatable and measured; **partner rather than compete** | Channel |

## Pricing reference points

Glean does not publish prices; third-party estimates put it around $45–75 per user per month with reported annual
minimums of roughly $50–60k ([Glean pricing estimates (third-party, eesel.ai)](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/glean-ai-pricing), [Glean pricing explained (third-party, GoSearch)](https://www.gosearch.ai/blog/glean-pricing-explained)). Treat as unverified. Prices of the other
alternatives were not researched; do not quote them until they are.
