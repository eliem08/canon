# Demo script (12 minutes, synthetic US company)

Every command below was run on 2026-10-31 against the committed synthetic US dataset (`data/synthetic_west`). Run them
once before the call:

```bash
uv sync
uv run canon pilot --data data/synthetic_west --out out_west --as-of 2026-10-01
```

**Rule for the call:** this is a synthetic company we generated. Say so at the start. Never present a number from it as
a client result. Prebuilt copies of the outputs are in `docs/sales/samples/`.

## 0. Frame it (1 min)
"Your agents will answer from Salesforce, Slack and your contracts. I'll show you what goes wrong in a typical company's
data and what we deliver in two weeks. This is a made-up software vendor with 25 customers, generated by us."

## 1. The mess (1 min)
Harbor Foods has two CRM accounts: "Harbor - HQ" (owner Priya Patel, 1,190,000) and "Harbor Foods Corp" (owner
David Kim, 1,468,000). Open the signed contract `data/synthetic_west/contracts/CN-2024-142_v2.txt`: **USD 1,468,000**,
and Section 12 says it supersedes version 1. "Which value, and which owner, should an agent give?"

## 2. The audit (4 min): the deliverable
Open `out_west/index.html` (about one second to produce, on a laptop, with no network):
- **Headline:** 59 stale CRM values, 16 customers with duplicate CRM accounts, 6 merges to confirm, 6 renewals in the
  next 180 days.
- **`crm_fixes.csv`:** the first lines are Harbor Foods. The CRM owner is *Priya Patel*, suggested *David Kim*. The
  contract value is *1,190,000*, suggested *1,468,000*, with `contract:CN-2024-142_v2` as evidence. Record ids and
  column names are the CRM's own, so the admin can bulk-update after review.
- **Owner changes seen only in Slack:** e.g. Redwood Capital Partners, *Marcus Johnson* → *Rachel Green*.
- **`renewals.csv`:** Cedar Insurance Brokers renews on **2026-12-01**, auto-renews, and the last day to give notice
  is **2026-11-01**. "That's the line that saves a renewal."
- **`review.html`:** two records side by side, *same / different / not sure*; and **Correct a value**, signed with the
  person's name. "We don't guess on borderline cases. Your account owners decide, and it sticks on every run."
- **Quarantine:** values Canon would not read safely are listed with what to do, never guessed.

## 3. Same question, different people (4 min): permissions
```bash
uv run python examples/mcp_client.py --west "Harbor Foods"
```
- **RevOps admin:** contract value 1,468,000 from the active contract. The stale 1,190,000 is shown as an
  alternative, with sources cited.
- **Sales rep (Sarah Chen):** sees the CRM view only, flagged *some sources are restricted for this caller*. She
  never sees the contract.
- **Legal (Dana Whitfield):** sees the contract, not the account owner.
- **Intern:** "No customer found."
- Say: "An agent built on the raw export doesn't know who is asking. On this synthetic data, strong retrieval exposed
  restricted records in 87% of answers. Canon did in none of 4,400." (`docs/reports/permission_report_west.md`)

## 4. The honest numbers (1 min)
- Accuracy vs a well-built RAG: **about the same**. Say it plainly (`docs/reports/eval_summary.md`).
- The differences that matter: permissions enforced before scoring, a source for every fact, and a fix list.
- On public real data: `docs/sales/PROOF.md`. "We'll measure all of this on *your* data. That's what the audit is."

## 5. The ask (1 min)
"Two weeks, your exports, up to 30 questions your team writes. It runs in your environment. You get the fix list,
the renewal calendar, the permission test, and a week-2 report." Send `docs/DATA_REQUEST.md` and
`docs/sales/PILOT_PROPOSAL.md` after the call.

## If they ask
- **"Does it work on our data?"** Unknown until we run it; that's the point of the audit.
- **"Which AI do you use?"** None for ingestion and resolution. For the optional before/after test: whichever model you
  approve, including one you host.
- **"Why not just build RAG?"** You can, and it will be about as accurate. It won't know who is asking, give one
  reconciled value per fact with its source, or tell RevOps what is stale in the CRM.
- **"SOC 2?"** Not yet. It runs in your environment with networking off, writes nothing to your systems, and deletes
  everything at the end with a receipt (`docs/product/SECURITY_REVIEW.md`).
