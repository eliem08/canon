# Canon: one-page product brief

*Sales material. Prices are hypotheses to validate in discovery; no customer, partnership or certification exists yet. Never present synthetic-data numbers as client results. Updated 2026-10-30.*

**Canon: Customer-Data & AI-Readiness Audit**

**The problem.** Customer facts live in your CRM, contracts and WhatsApp/Teams conversations, and they disagree.
An AI assistant connected to them repeats stale values and may show people documents they shouldn't see.

**What we do in 2 weeks, inside your network.** No data leaves your servers unless you choose otherwise.
- Read your CRM export, contracts (PDF, Word, scans; French, Arabic, English) and chat exports.
- Find duplicate customers, CRM values that conflict with signed contracts or newer sources, and values we
  could not read safely. We refuse to guess, and tell you why.
- List contracts renewing in the next 6 months, with the last day to give notice where the contract states it.
- Give you `crm_fixes.csv` in your CRM's own record ids, ready for a bulk update by your admin.
- Optional: show an AI assistant answering from the cleaned facts with sources, limited to what each role may see.

**What you get.** A report (HTML/PDF), fix files, a review page for your account owners, and a follow-up report
showing what changed after your fixes.

**What we need from you.** Exports, a machine to run on, and about 3–4 hours of your team's time.

**What we don't do (yet).** We don't write to your CRM, sync live, or offer a hosted service. Contract dates that
depend on events ("one year after launch") aren't computed. Results on your data are measured during the audit;
we don't promise them in advance.

**Evidence so far.** Tests on public datasets (US SEC contracts, a French company registry, scanned receipts)
and on synthetic data. Reports are available on request. Your audit is the first measurement on your data.
