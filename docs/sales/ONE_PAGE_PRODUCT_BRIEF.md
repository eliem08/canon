# Canon: customer-data and AI-readiness audit

*Sales material for US, UK and EU buyers. Prices are hypotheses to validate in discovery; no customer, partnership or certification exists yet. Never present synthetic-data numbers as client results. Updated 2026-10-31.*

**The problem.** Customer facts live in your CRM, your contracts and your Slack or Teams channels, and they
disagree. An AI agent connected to them repeats stale values, confuses duplicate accounts, and can show people
records they shouldn't see.

**What we do in two weeks, inside your environment.** Your data does not leave your infrastructure unless you choose
a hosted model for the optional evaluation.

- Read your CRM export (Salesforce, HubSpot, CSV/XLSX), contract folders (PDF, Word, scans) and Slack/Teams exports.
- Find duplicate customers, CRM values contradicted by signed contracts or newer sources, and values we could not
  read safely. We refuse to guess, and say why.
- List contracts renewing in the next six months, with the last day to give notice where the contract states it.
- Deliver `crm_fixes.csv` in your CRM's own record ids, ready for your admin's bulk update after review.
- Optional: show an AI agent (over MCP) answering from the cleaned facts with sources, limited to what each role may see.

**What you get.** A dashboard and report, fix files, a review page for account owners (confirm merges, correct
values), a renewal calendar, a week-2 report of what changed, and a deletion receipt at the end.

**What we need from you.** Exports, a machine or VM to run on, about 3–4 hours of your team's time.

**What we don't do (yet).** No CRM write-back, no live sync, no hosted service, no SOC 2 report. Contract dates
that depend on events ("12 months after go-live") are not computed. Results on your data are measured during the
audit; we don't promise them in advance.

**Evidence so far.** Public real-data tests (US SEC contracts, US business listings, scanned receipts) and synthetic
data; reports on request (`docs/sales/PROOF.md`). Your audit is the first measurement on your data.
