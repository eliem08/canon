# Pricing and packaging (hypotheses)

*Every price here is an untested hypothesis, in Moroccan dirhams (MAD; about 10.8 MAD per euro, check the day's rate).
No customer has paid any of these amounts. Packages only include what the product does today (see
`docs/product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`). Updated 2026-10-30.*

| Package | What it includes (all exists today) | Not included | Price hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Demo** (free) | 30-minute walkthrough on the synthetic company; the sample audit files | Any client data | Free |
| **Design-partner audit** | Two-week audit (`canon pilot`), review loop, renewal calendar, week-2 comparison, deletion receipt; first 2–3 clients only, against a testimonial and permission to publish anonymised results | Agent trial | 25–40k MAD one-off |
| **Standard audit** | As above, plus the optional agent trial on up to 3 roles and a before/after eval on the client's questions (with a client-approved model) | Live sync, CRM write-back | 60–90k MAD one-off |
| **Recurring readiness** | Monthly or quarterly re-audit with `--compare`, agent server for named users, support by email/WhatsApp (1 business day) | Hosted service | 8–15k MAD / month, annual |
| **Custom connector** | A new export format or CRM preset (e.g. Dynamics), with fixtures and tests | — | Quoted per connector |

## Assumptions behind the numbers

- **Value.** A single missed auto-renewal notice or a quarter of wrong owner assignments plausibly costs more than the
  audit for a 100–1,000 employee B2B company. *Not validated: ask for the prospect's own figures in discovery.*
- **Cost to deliver.** About 3–5 founder-days per audit on site or remote; no infrastructure cost (runs on the client's
  machine); software cost near zero.
- **Buying behaviour.** Mid-market buyers in the region buy a fixed-price deliverable more easily than a subscription
  for an unproven tool; the recurring offer comes after a successful audit.
- **Competitor pricing.** Not researched yet; do not quote competitor prices.

## Validate before relying on it

Ask 5 prospects for a reaction to the standard price in discovery (`docs/sales/OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md`). Adjust if
fewer than 3 consider it plausible.
