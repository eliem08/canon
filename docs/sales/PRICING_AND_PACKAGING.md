# Pricing and packaging (hypotheses)

*Sales material for US, UK and EU buyers. Prices are hypotheses to validate in discovery; no customer, partnership or certification exists yet. Never present synthetic-data numbers as client results. Updated 2026-10-31.*

Every price is an untested hypothesis in US dollars. No customer has paid any of these amounts. Packages include only
what the product does today (`docs/product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`).

| Package | Includes (all exists today) | Not included | Price hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Demo** | 30-minute walkthrough on the synthetic US company (`data/synthetic_west`) | Client data | Free |
| **Design-partner audit** | Two-week audit, review loop, renewal calendar, week-2 comparison, deletion receipt; first 2–3 clients, against a reference | Agent trial | $15–25k one-off |
| **Standard audit** | As above + agent trial on up to 3 roles + before/after evaluation on the client's questions | Live sync, write-back | $35–50k one-off |
| **Recurring readiness** | Monthly or quarterly re-audit with `--compare`, agent server for named users, support (1 business day) | Hosted service, SOC 2 | $2–5k / month, annual |
| **Custom connector** | New export format or CRM preset (e.g. Dynamics 365), with fixtures and tests | — | Quoted |

## Assumptions

- **Value.** A single missed auto-renewal notice or a quarter of misassigned accounts plausibly costs a 200–2,000
  employee B2B company more than the audit. *Not validated: ask for the prospect's numbers in discovery.*
- **Reference points.** Enterprise search is reported (third-party estimates, unverified) at about $45–75 per user per
  month with annual minimums around $50–60k (`docs/product/COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`). The audit is priced as a
  fixed-scope project below that annual commitment.
- **Cost to deliver.** About 3–5 founder-days per audit; no infrastructure cost (runs on the client's machine).
- **Buying behaviour.** Mid-market RevOps budgets approve fixed-price projects faster than new platforms; the
  subscription follows a successful audit.

## Validate before relying on it

Get a reaction to the standard price from 5 prospects in discovery. Adjust if fewer than 3 find it plausible.
